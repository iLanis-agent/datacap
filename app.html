How much of your monthly data cap will streaming eat, and which quality fits?
Data: Netflix Help Center, "How to control how much data Netflix uses". Per hour, per device, "up to": Low 0.3 GB, Medium 0.7 GB, High standard definition 1 GB, High HD 3 GB, High 4K 7 GB. These are maximums, so real use is often lower, and Auto quality changes with your connection. Figures are Netflix only: other services, downloads and everything else on your connection are not included. 1 GB is taken as 1,000 MB and 1 TB as 1,000 GB. Mbps is the equivalent steady rate for that GB per hour. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
